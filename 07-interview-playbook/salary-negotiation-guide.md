# Senior Salary Negotiation & 90-Day Notice Period Playbook

---

## 1. How to Break the "30% Hike on Current CTC" Trap

When your current CTC is lower than your true market value (e.g. ₹4.5 LPA while market is ₹15–20 LPA), standard HRs will try to offer only 30%–40% (~₹6–7 LPA).

### The Battle-Tested Script:
> **Recruiter:** *"What is your current CTC and expected CTC?"*  
>  
> **You:** *"My current fixed CTC at RapidData is ₹5 LPA because I was hired through an early career baseline, but my scope of work quickly escalated to Senior Developer and Module Lead, where I architected mission-critical platforms for the Government of Dubai and KPMG. I also hold Siemens Mendix Advanced Certification, write custom Core Java microservices, and build decoupled React frontends.*  
>  
> *Because of this specialized full-stack skillset and my 100% on-time go-live record across 12 production apps, I am seeking a market-aligned package of **₹16 to ₹20 LPA**, which reflects the standard industry band for an Advanced Mendix & Full-Stack Lead."*

## 2. The 3-Offer Notice Period Escalator

```
[ Day 0: Resign on Mar 18 ] ──> Lock in Offer #1 (₹10 - ₹12 LPA) from Big 4 / Service MNC
                                      |
                                      v
[ Day 45: Serving Notice ]  ──> Use Offer #1 as baseline with GCCs/Consultancies ──> Offer #2 (₹14 - ₹16 LPA)
                                      |
                                      v
[ Day 75: <15 Days Left ]   ──> "Immediate Joiner" bidding war with Product / Platinum Partners ──> Final Offer (₹18 - ₹22+ LPA)
```

### Why This Works:
Recruiters lose candidates every day to counter-offers. A verified candidate who has only **15 days of notice left** is pure gold in the market. Companies will pay the maximum cap of their budget to secure you immediately.

## 3. The UAE / Dubai Direct Route

- Dubai employers **do not ask for Indian salary slips** to calculate percentage hikes.
- They budget in UAE Dirhams:
  - Standard Senior Mendix / React Engineer: **AED 18,000 – AED 25,000 / month** (Tax-Free).
  - Equivalent to **₹4.5L – ₹6.2L per month** (₹55L – ₹75L INR annualized).
- Highlight your **KHDA Dubai (Government of Dubai)** project with dynamic Arabic RTL compliance. It provides immediate credibility that you understand UAE regulatory and enterprise standards.
